# Privacy Policy for Junior Detective

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game **Junior Detective**, a logic puzzle for kids and
grown-ups, handles your information. The short version: **the app does
not collect, send or share any personal data.**

## What the app does not collect

- The app has **no permission to access the internet**, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- It has **no accounts** and no sign-in. You never enter your name, email address or any other
  personal details.
- It contains **no advertising, no analytics, no tracking** and no third-party services that collect
  data.
- It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- It has no in-app purchases.

## What is stored on your device

To work properly, the game saves a small amount of information **only on your device**, in the app's
private storage:

- your game progress and records;
- your settings, such as whether sound is turned on.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we (the developer) never receive it
and have no access to it.

The app opts out of Android's automatic backup, so this game data is not copied into your Google
account backup.

## Sharing a puzzle number

When you choose "Share puzzle", the app opens the standard Android share menu with a short text
containing the puzzle number. You decide which app (if any) to send it with. The app itself sends
nothing. Whatever you share is then handled by the app you chose, under that app's privacy policy.

## How long the data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's
storage (Android **Settings > Apps > Junior Detective > Storage > Clear storage**; menu names vary a little
between phones) or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for
us to delete on our side.

## Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Since nothing
is sent over a network, there is no data in transit to protect.

## Children's privacy

Junior Detective is designed for children as well as adults. We do not collect personal information from
anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The app has no
ads, no chat, no social features, no accounts and no internet access, so a child cannot share personal
information through it. Parents or guardians who have questions can contact us at the address below.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the
